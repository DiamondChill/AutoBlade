How to play

Each round, four chords from C major play, one per bar. They come from the first six degrees of the scale:

I
C C E G
ii
Dm D F A
iii
Em E G B
IV
F F A C
V
G G B D
vi
Am A C E

Enter a numeral for each bar (1 for I, 2 for ii, and so on), then submit. You get four guesses. After each one the bars change colour:

  • VRight chord in the right bar.
  • VThe chord is in the progression, in a different bar.
  • VThe chord isn’t in the progression, or you used it more times than it appears.

The same chord never plays in two bars in a row, but it can come back later. Replay the progression as often as you like, and use the play button beside a past guess to hear how it compares.

Keyboard: 1–6 enter a chord, Backspace deletes, Enter submits, Space plays or stops, T plays the I chord.
